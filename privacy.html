Privacy Policy

Updated on October 29, 2026. Version 1.0.

Zenit is a healthy-habits app shaped like a game: water, meals, sleep and workouts turn into XP, and you can compete with friends in rooms. This policy explains, in plain language, what data Zenit uses, what for, and your rights under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018) and other privacy laws that apply to you.

1. Who is responsible

Zenit is developed by Ryan Lucas, who is the data controller. Contact for any privacy matter: Zenit's contact email (coming soon).

2. What data we use

  • Account: email and password, if you create an account. The password is stored securely by Google Firebase; we have no access to it.
  • Profile: name or nickname, chosen mascot and app preferences.
  • Health and habit data: starting weight, goal and weigh-ins, water, meals, calories, sleep, workouts, loads and measurements you log. These are sensitive data and are only used with your consent.
  • Photos: progress and machine photos stay only on your device. Photos you send in a room's chat are visible to the people in it.
  • Chat and rooms: messages, reactions, challenges (1v1) and votes you make in a room.
  • Technical data: device security checks (Firebase App Check and reCAPTCHA) to prevent abuse.

3. What we use it for

  • Making the app work: calculating XP, levels, streaks, goals and leaderboards.
  • Saving your progress in the cloud and keeping all your devices in sync, if you have an account.
  • Showing the leaderboard and chat to the people in the same room as you.
  • Generating assistant replies, counting calories from plate photos, reading workout plans, building meal plans and checking challenge proofs, when you use these features.

Zenit does not sell your data, does not show ads and does not use your data for advertising.

4. What your room sees

People in the same room see your name, mascot, XP, level, streak, the day's habits (water, meals, workout, sleep) and the percentage of your weight progress. Your actual weight never shows to the room. In the chat, everyone in the room sees the messages and photos sent there.

5. Who we share it with

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): sign-in, cloud database and app security. Servers may be located outside your country, with the safeguards required by law.
  • Google Gemini, through Firebase AI Logic: when you use the assistant, plate photo, workout plan reading, meal plan, proof checking or natural voices, the necessary text or image is sent to Google only to generate that answer.
  • Authorities, only when required by law.

6. How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your cloud data is deleted right away; the provider's technical backup copies may take up to 30 days to disappear. Messages you sent in other people's rooms are deleted too.

7. Your rights

You can, at any time: confirm and access your data, correct it, download a copy (portability), withdraw your consent and request deletion. In the app: Settings → Download my data and Settings → Delete my account. Also by email at Zenit's contact email (coming soon).

8. How to delete your account

In the app: Settings → Delete my account, and confirm with your password. Everything is deleted: cloud progress, room participation and messages. If you can't get into the app, send an email to Zenit's contact email (coming soon) with the subject "Delete account", from your account's email, and we'll delete it within 15 days.

9. Age

Zenit is intended for people 18 or older.

10. Security

We use encrypted connections, access rules in the database (each person can only read and change what is theirs and what belongs to the rooms they're in) and device verification against abuse. No system is 100% secure; if something happens, we'll notify whoever is affected.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the app lets you know and asks for your acceptance again.

Terms of Use

  • Respect in rooms. Sending offensive content, hate, harassment, threats, nudity or sexual content, scams or spam is forbidden. You can report messages and AI answers and block people. The room owner can delete messages and remove people from the room.
  • Moderation. Reports are reviewed by the Zenit team. Content that breaks these rules is removed, and accounts may be suspended or deleted.
  • Room prizes are agreed among the participants. Zenit does not provide, guarantee or take responsibility for prizes.
  • Your account is personal. Keep your password safe.
  • The app is provided "as is". We'll do our best for it to work well, but there may be bugs or interruptions.
  • These terms are governed by Brazilian law.
